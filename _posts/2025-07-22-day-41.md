what_i_learned: >
   Today, we worked on our 90 second pitch. We had to take our project which is something we’ve been working on in-depth and translate it into something simple enough for anyone to understand, without losing its value or meaning. It was very straightforward and it was not hard deciding what message we wanted to convey as far as the tone and what we want people to take out of our website. Collaborating with the team helped strengthen the pitch. Each person brought a different perspective, which helped clarify our language and refine our message. Not to mention, sticking to 90 seconds was harder than I expected. It taught me how to pace our delivery and be strategic with our word choice to make every second count.

  Today was a productive and eye-opening day as we began crafting our 90-second pitch. Starting the pitch forced me to really think about the heart of our project and how to communicate it clearly and confidently in such a short amount of time. It was a challenge to narrow everything down and decide what truly mattered most to highlight, but it helped me realize how powerful concise storytelling can be. I learned the importance of having a strong hook, staying within the time limit, and making sure the delivery feels natural and engaging.
