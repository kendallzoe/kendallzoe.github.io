what_i_learned: >
Today, I got to experience just how far technology has come in connecting humans and machines during a workshop with Dr. Chris S. Crawford from the University of Alabama. We talked about Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) but not just the usual stuff like keyboards and screens, but more advanced ways we can use our bodies and even our brains to control technology. One of the most exciting parts was getting to move a robot using muscle signals and flying a drone using just brain waves.

reflection: >
Reflecting on today’s workshop with Dr. Chris S. Crawford, I feel genuinely inspired by the direction technology is heading, especially in the field of Human-Computer Interaction. Getting to control a robot with my muscles and fly a drone using brain waves made the concept of mind-body interfaces feel incredibly real and exciting. It was more than just a demonstration; it was a glimpse into a future where our thoughts and movements can directly shape the digital world around us. What stood out to me most was how these innovations are not just cool gadgets but tools with the potential to improve lives, especially for people with disabilities or mobility challenges.
