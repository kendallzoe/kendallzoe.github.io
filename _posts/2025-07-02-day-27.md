what_i_learned: >
   Today,  we wrapped our my PowerPoint slides and finally got to share them with our mentors and teachers. After presenting what I had so far, they pointed out that some of my slides were a bit too wordy and could benefit from being more visual and concise. It was helpful to get that outside perspective because I realized that while everything made sense to me, it might not come across as clearly to others. I took notes on their suggestions and started thinking about ways to simplify my content without losing important information. I definitely learned more about presenting and finessing your way through a presentation without having so many words on one slide!



blockers: No blockers.

reflection: >
  Reflecting on today, I’m really grateful for the feedback I received. I was initially upset because I thought they were not grasping what the presentation entails. But, sometimes when you're so deep into a project, it's easy to miss how it looks to someone seeing it for the first time. Hearing that my slides were too wordy made me take a step back and think about how I communicate my ideas that less can really be more. It reminded me that clarity and simplicity often have more impact than trying to include everything at once. I'm learning to not take critique personally but instead use it as a tool to grow and improve. This process has pushed me to be more intentional with how I present information and to keep the audience experience in mind.
