what_i_learned: >
   Today’s workshop from the Writing Center really helped me understand not only how to write a research paper, but also how to present it effectively. One of the most valuable parts was the section on presentation tips. I learned how important it is to know my audience, speak clearly and confidently, and use visuals or slides to support my key points—not overwhelm them. They also emphasized practicing ahead of time and timing myself so that I stay within the limit while still covering everything I need to. Another major takeaway was the review of research methodology. We went over how the methodology section should clearly explain how I conducted my research like what methods I used, why I chose them, and how they help answer my research question.

reflection: >
  Reflecting on today’s work, I feel much more confident about both writing and presenting a research paper. Before the session, I had a general idea of what a research paper involved, but the workshop really broke down the process in a way that made it feel more manageable. I appreciated the review of methodology, learning how to explain my research process clearly and justify my choices made me realize how important that section is to the overall integrity of the paper. The presentation tips were also a game-changer for me. I’ve always been a little nervous about presenting, but hearing practical advice like practicing in front of others, staying on time, and keeping visuals simple and relevant gave me a better strategy for preparing.
